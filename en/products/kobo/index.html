KOBO.

Specialist plugins, being made in our workshop.

KOBO, from the Japanese word kōbō, a workshop, is the family of plugins we are building for ISHIZUE. Each is organised around one piece of work a person needs to finish. None is released yet.

Talk to us

Design rules

Three rules each app is being built to meet before release.

  1. 1

    No model of its own

    The thinking stays in ISHIZUE, with the models the organisation chose.

  2. 2

    One way in

    Each app connects to ISHIZUE as an MCP tool bundle.

  3. 3

    Offline until allowed

    Network use stays off until someone allows it.

The six apps

Six apps, each named for its craft.

Each name is a Japanese word for the work the app does. All six are in development and cannot be installed yet.

  • EMAKI

    From emaki, an illustrated handscroll.

    Being built for presentations, from an outline to slides.

    In development

  • SOROBAN

    From soroban, the abacus.

    Being built for spreadsheets.

    In development

  • FUDE

    Being built for Word documents.

    In development

  • HANKO

    From hanko, the personal seal.

    Being built for PDF forms, seals and redaction.

    In development

  • MEKIKI

    From mekiki, an appraiser’s trained eye.

    In development

  • MITATE

How they are made

Made the way a partner product is made.

How a partner product joins

  • Each KOBO app is being built to ship as an ISHIZUE bundle: an MCP server and one configuration entry.
  • Each is being built to do deterministic work and leave the thinking to ISHIZUE’s agent and the models the customer runs.
  • Each is being built to keep network use off until allowed and to block third-party hosts by default.
