SHINRAI.

From the Japanese word shinrai, trust.

One platform: ISHIZUE at its base, KOBO plugins, and room for partners.

SHINRAI is our platform for AI that runs on the computers and servers an organisation already controls. ISHIZUE is the AI harness at its base, and KOBO is the family of specialist plugins we are building for it in our workshop.

Talk to us

The layers

Four layers, assembled the same way for every organisation.

  1. People at work

    The teams who use it. The interface is in Japanese and English.

  2. AppsIn development

    KOBO plugins from our workshop, and products made by partners, joining as ISHIZUE plugins or MCP servers. KOBO plugins are being built to make no model calls of their own: ISHIZUE’s agent calls their tools.

  3. ISHIZUEDeveloper preview

    The harness that brings models, routing, agents, memory and permissions together and keeps the session log. Every part of it is a plugin, including the agent loop.

  4. The organisation’s hardware

    Computers, office workstations and on-premises servers it already controls. ISHIZUE needs no Shristyverse cloud service.

Set-ups

Three ways to set it up.

  1. One computer

    The desktop app and the model apps on the same machine.

  2. Computers and one workstation

    Models on one approved workstation on the private network, over HTTPS. Auto tries it first. A failure before answering moves the request once to the computer’s own models.

  3. Behind a gateway

    A gateway in front of the organisation’s own model servers, paired as Auto’s workstation; the gateway then chooses among its models.

The web interface accepts connections only from the same computer (127.0.0.1), so it is not a shared office server.

Editions

How an edition is assembled.

Our standard layers at the base, the customer’s own layer above them, and partner products on top. An update replaces our layers and leaves the customer’s in place.
