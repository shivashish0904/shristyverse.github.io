Shristyverse GK is an AI company in Okegawa, Saitama. We build SHINRAI, a local-first AI platform, so that organisations can use AI they understand and control.

Mission

Make AI that organisations can trust, because they can see where it runs and how it decides.

Trust in AI should not rest on a promise. It should rest on things you can check: which machine answered, why that model was chosen, what was remembered and who approved each change.

So we build carefully. We test with real tasks, write down what fails, and label every feature with its status.

The SHINRAI platform

Three names, one platform.

Our products carry Japanese words, written in English letters. Each name says what the part is for.

  • SHINRAI

    From 信頼 (shinrai), trust.

    The platform as a whole: everything we build so that people can rely on the AI they work with. ISHIZUE and KOBO are part of SHINRAI.

  • ISHIZUE

    From 礎 (ishizue), a foundation stone.

    The AI harness at the base. It brings models, agents, memory and permissions together on your hardware.

  • KOBO

    From 工房 (kōbō), a workshop.

    The family of specialist apps and plugins that do the craft work on top of ISHIZUE.
