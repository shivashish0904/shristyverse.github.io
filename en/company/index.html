Company

Trust, built on a foundation.

Shristyverse GK builds SHINRAI, a local-first AI platform, so that organisations can use AI they understand and control.

Mission

Make AI that organisations can trust, because they can see where it runs and how it decides.

Trust in AI should not rest on a promise. It should rest on things you can check: which machine answered, why that model was chosen, what was remembered and who approved each change.

The names

Each name is a Japanese word.

  • SHINRAI

    From the Japanese word shinrai, trust.

    The platform as a whole: what we build so that organisations can rely on the AI they work with. ISHIZUE and KOBO are part of it.

  • ISHIZUE

    From ishizue, a foundation stone.

    The AI harness at the base. It brings models, routing, agents, memory and permissions together on hardware the organisation controls.

  • KOBO

    From kōbō, a workshop.

    The plugins we are building in our workshop, to do specialist work on ISHIZUE.
