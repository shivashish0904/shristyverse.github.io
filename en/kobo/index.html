工房

KOBO · 工房 · workshop

A workshop for
every kind of work.

The name comes from 工房 (kōbō), a workshop. KOBO is the family of apps and plugins built for ISHIZUE, each organised around a piece of work a person needs to finish. This catalogue shows what ISHIZUE can do today and what we are building.

What the statuses mean

Developer preview
Included in the current ISHIZUE developer preview. Not a general release.
In development
Being built and tested. Not yet installable.
Exploring
An area we are researching. No product yet.

Every KOBO app follows three rules

  1. 01

    No model of its own

    The thinking stays in ISHIZUE, with the models you chose.

  2. 02

    One way in

    Each app connects to ISHIZUE as an MCP tool bundle.

  3. 03

    Offline until allowed

    Network use stays off until you enable it.
